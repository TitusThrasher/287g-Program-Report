# ICE's Contract Language

The 287(g) documents are written in government procurement language and 'legalese', which can obscure what is actually being described. All terms and clauses referenced below come from the Task Force Model MOA revision dated **August 19, 2025** — check the revision date on any copy you obtain for versioning purposes.

---

## Contract Vocabulary + Common Language

| ‘service provider’ | The participating law enforcement agency (LEA). Reframes the agency as a vendor delivering a contracted service to a federal customer — in this case, that’s ICE / DHS. |
| :---- | :---- |
| **‘credentialed TFO’** | A Task Force Officer (TFO) is any LEO employed by an agency participating in the Task Force Model 287(g), who has completed ICE's online training and is authorized to act on ICE’s behalf.  Reporting obligations are tied to each credentialed officer, which would imply participating agencies are documenting per-officer records of TFO-invoiced hours somewhere. |
| **‘apprehension’** | Used in the service agreement's reporting clause. Note where it appears and where it doesn’t — like the UAC incentives document, which describes the same work with different language. |
| **‘safety verification’** | The UAC incentives document uses this term to describe locating a minor and confirming their living situation and school enrollment. **Not an arrest**. The gap between this phrase and "apprehension"in the service agreement is important, and it may be worth quoting both rather than paraphrasing them. |
| **‘Unaccompanied alien children’ (UAC)** | ICE's statutory term. Definitionally narrowed in the service agreement to cases "specifically designated by ICE" — meaning ICE controls which children are on the list, and the list itself is **not public**. |
| **‘unaccounted for’** | Does not mean missing. It means released from federal custody and not currently tracked by the agency. Officials have used the ambiguity to imply the former. |
| **‘incentive’ / ‘reimbursement’ /  ‘grant‘** | Three different things, but conflated in usage. A reimbursement repays a documented cost;  a grant is awarded upfront against a budget (in this case, largely through state boards \+ state comptroller grant programs); and an incentive is paid on completion of an outcome (in this case, quota-based financial bonuses for certain Task Force program work, like the UAC Safety Verification Incentive (see below). |
| **‘incorporated by reference**’ | Pulls an external document into the contract without reproducing it. This is the hinge that makes an unsigned attachment binding — like the [UAC Safety Verification Incentives](https://wehco.media.clients.ellingtoncms.com/news/document/2026/07/10/CTFP-UAC_Safety_Verification_Initiative_Incentives_Program_for_287g_Partners.pdf) document — and a phrase to consider searching for when you suspect another contract layer may exist. |

## Specific Contract Language

Passages from the documents that have surfaced, as quoted:

1. **Scope of UAC work** — [287(g) service agreement](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/28083186-287g-service-agreement-ocso-2/), Owyhee County Sheriff's Office (Idaho):

> "any case specifically designated by ICE that was previously in Office of Refugee Resettlement custody, subsequently released, and unaccounted for."

2. **Reporting obligation** — same [287(g) service agreement](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/28083186-287g-service-agreement-ocso-2/):

> "The service provider shall provide ERO with hours spent performing ICE enforcement activities for adult and Unaccompanied Alien Children (UAC), for each credentialed TFO assigned to the apprehension."

3. [**UAC Safety Verification Initiative,** Incentives Program for 287(g) Partners](https://wehco.media.clients.ellingtoncms.com/news/document/2026/07/10/CTFP-UAC_Safety_Verification_Initiative_Incentives_Program_for_287g_Partners.pdf):

> "Two incentive categories are available to agencies participating in the initiative. When combined, they amount to $22,500 in incentives per UAC case, depending on the scope of the work activities completed for each UAC case."

# Federal FOIA

File with ICE for program-level records. Do *not* expect it to be your fastest route to any individual agency's contract documents.

---

## Filing a request

287(g) program records — agreements, correspondence about negotiation or cancellation, referral and arrest data, training materials given to participating agencies — are held by ICE. 

Filing system: [SecureRelease Portal](https://www.securerelease.us/). 

Once you’ve created an account:

1. **Agency:** Department of Homeland Security   
2. **Component:** U.S. Immigration and Customs and Enforcement (ICE)   
3. Scroll down and click  ‘Next’ button  
4. **Select type of requester** (may not be editable for existing SecureRelease profiles)  
5. Scroll down and click ‘Confirm’ button  
6. **Request Details Page:**  
   1. **Request Type:** ‘FOIA Request’  
   2. Paste full request details in ‘**Request Description**’ text field  
   3. **Request Designation**: ‘Other (I am not requesting records about a person)’

**Statutory clock:** 20 business days to respond; 30 where records are in field offices or 'voluminous'.

**Actual timeline:** 6–18 months, or longer, for non-expedited institutional requests.

**Appeal window:** 90 days from an adverse determination, filed to the **Office of Principal Legal Advisor**, Government Information Law Division.

**Before you file:** 

* Check [ICE's FOIA Library](https://www.ice.gov/foia/library?field_foia_category_target_id=All&combine=287%28g%29&created=&sort_by=created) — some pre-2025 287(g) archived materials are posted there.  
* [SecureRelease Reading Room](https://www.securerelease.us/public-reading-room)

## Fee category and waiver

***Fee Waiver Justification:*** As a journalist, you should qualify as a news media requester: no search or review fees, and the first 100 pages of duplication free.

Separately, request a fee waiver on public-interest grounds — describe your outlet/publication, your audience, and how you will publish the released records. The two arguments are independent and may be useful to make together.

---

## Expedited processing

Two of the four available statutory grounds may apply: urgency to inform the public about federal government activity, where the requester is primarily engaged in disseminating information; and widespread and exceptional media interest affecting public confidence in government.

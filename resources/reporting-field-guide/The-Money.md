## 2️⃣ ‘BIDEN’ Grant Program (DOJ)

When: Created in **July 2025** via OBBBA; applications accepted starting **summer 2026**.

Along with enormous ICE/DHS funding, the OBBBA also authorized a **$3 billion** [DOJ grant program](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/24/nx-s1-5886181/justice-department-is-offering-billions-in-new-grants-for-police-who-partner-with-ice) for law enforcement agencies, with eligibility tied to 287(g) participation (and/or other federal task force program), and with [multiple application rounds](https://bja.ojp.gov/funding/opportunities/DOJ-FY26-BIDEN-program). 

See [§61101. Bridging Immigration-related Deficits Experienced Nationwide Reimbursement Fund](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section61101&num=0&edition=prelim).

## 3️⃣ State Grant Programs

Several states run parallel state-level grant programs. My program report includes a separate tracking of state-level grant awards, totaling over **$127 million** in confirmed grants (and over \$330 million earmarked) across just three states.

See [/data/financials](/data/financials) for financial award records and program details.

---

### Tennessee

An under-reported [**$75 million** grant program](https://newschannel9.com/news/local/new-documents-show-how-hamilton-county-training-center-287g-program-came-together) for county sheriffs, managed by the state’s Office of Criminal Justice Programs.  
  * eligibility requires the agency to demonstrate it “has taken all necessary steps to enter into a 287(g) agreement with the federal government.”  
  * see [Grant Solicitation Packet](https://newschannel9.com/resources/pdf/458d91d2-d0ae-421f-bb32-e61b31cd1e02-FY26VCIFZipCodesTnGrants.pdf), published by **WTVC** (May 22, 2026)

---

### Texas

Grants are available to county law enforcement agencies through the state [comptroller’s office, under **SB 8**](https://comptroller.texas.gov/economy/development/grants/sb8/), and the governor's office, under [**Operation Lone Star**](https://txilc.org/txilc-program/operation-lone-star/).
  * Full details \+ documentation on the [Sheriff Immigration Law Enforcement Grant Program](https://comptroller.texas.gov/economy/development/grants/).
  * Operation Lone Star (OLS) is a local law enforcement + DHS collaboration tool unique to Texas, established by Gov. Greg Abbott in 2021. While 287(g) participation is not required as part of OLS, there is overlapping program participation in the state.

---

On Saturday, I wrote about ICE reaching a new high in 287(g) program participation, as the agency reached 2,000 active agreements last week.

Shortly after I published that post, Trump announced he was nominating ex-Oklahoma state trooper, Lance Schroyer, as ICE director. It looks like Schroyer, a friend of DHS secretary Markwayne Mullin, was recently hired by DHS after a long career with the Oklahoma state police.
